How we intend to work

What you can expect from us.

These are the working rules we follow once a scope is agreed.

Get a Free Consultation
01

Clarity

Say what the work is, what it is not, and who does it. Unconfirmed claims stay off the page and out of the proposal.

02

Agreed scope

A task is included when both sides have named it. Silence is not a way of adding work.

03

Steady communication

Questions, status, and changes have a place to go. The cadence is part of the engagement, not an afterthought.

04

Respect for clinical time

Ask the practice only for decisions and information that the scope actually needs. Do not collect patient records through a marketing form.

Healthcare revenue cycle support, agreed around the way your practice already works.
