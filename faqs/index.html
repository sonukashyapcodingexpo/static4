General

What does Saanvi Laboratories do?

Saanvi provides medical billing and revenue cycle management support. The exact tasks are confirmed with each practice.

Where are you based?

In Hyderabad, India. The street address is shared when a meeting is arranged.

Who do you work with?

Doctors, clinics, hospitals, and healthcare groups that need help with billing and related administration.

Services

Which services can I ask about?

Medical billing, transcription, coding, compliance reviews, practice consulting, and credentialing.

Do you work with my specialty?

We work with many specialties, including those listed on the specialties page. Ask us if yours is not shown.

How do we know what is included?

The tasks are written into the scope for your practice before work starts.

Working together

How does an engagement start?

With a consultation, then a written scope. A website visit by itself does not start the work.

What happens in the consultation?

We look at the workflow you want help with and say whether we can take it on.

Can timelines be guaranteed?

Timing is agreed for the specific work. It is part of the written scope.

Privacy

Can I send patient records?

No. The enquiry forms are for general questions. Do not submit patient records.

Where is the privacy policy?

It is linked in the footer and beside the enquiry form.
