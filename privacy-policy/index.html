Privacy policy.

This notice explains what happens when you send an enquiry from this website.

This notice explains how enquiries from this website are handled.

Who this covers

This notice covers the public website of Saanvi Laboratories, Hyderabad. It applies to enquiries you send about our services. It is not a notice for patient care, and this site is not a place to send patient records.

Information the forms ask for

Consultation and contact forms ask for details such as your name, practice name, specialty, phone, email, the service you are considering, and a message. You should not include patient names, clinical notes, insurance identifiers, or other sensitive health information.

How a future form service would use it

Enquiry details are used to reply to you and to keep a record of that conversation. They are not sold as a marketing list.

Cookies and analytics

This phase does not add analytics or advertising cookies. If tracking is added later, the notice and any required consent should be updated before it goes live.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent through the contact page. Do not include patient information.
