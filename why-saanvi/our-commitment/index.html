Our commitment

What we commit to in writing.

Quality checks, timing, and the way we report status are written into the scope for your practice.

Get a Free Consultation
01

Quality

Quality means the work matches the written scope and the review points you set. It is not a slogan and it is not a score.

02

Turnaround

Timing is discussed for the specific tasks. A marketing page is the wrong place for a guarantee, so none is offered.

03

Accuracy

Accuracy depends on the source information, the checks you want, and the people who approve exceptions. No accuracy percentage is published.

04

Transparency

Scope, status, and changes should be visible to the practice. If something is outside the agreement, it should be said plainly.
