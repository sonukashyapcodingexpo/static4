Why outsource

Hand a defined part of the billing work to us.

Clinics ask us to take billing work when it no longer fits the clinical day. What we take on is the work named in the scope.

Get a Free Consultation

What practices usually want from it

  • A place for billing tasks that no longer fits the clinical day.
  • A written owner for follow-up, instead of a shared inbox.
  • Room for in-house staff to stay on scheduling, patients, and decisions that should not leave the practice.

Cost, without a savings claim

Cost is a comparison you should make in the consultation: the fee for outside support, the staff time you would still spend, and the cost of rework when information is missing. We go through fees in the consultation rather than quoting a saving here.

Denials, as a workflow

A denial is a claim that needs a reason and a next action. Outsourcing can include tracking those items if the scope says so. It cannot promise a denial rate. The useful question is who notices the denial, who may act on it, and what information they need from the practice.

A fair test

Bring one workflow, not the whole practice history.

Describe the task that is hardest to staff. We can say whether it looks like a fit for a scoped engagement.
